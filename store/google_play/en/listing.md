# Title (≤30)
Extracter: Audio to Text

# Short description (≤80)
Turn any audio or video into text — offline, private, no account, no limits.

# Full description (≤4000)
Extracter turns recordings into text right on your phone. Lectures, meetings, interviews, podcasts, voice notes, videos: pick a file and the transcript appears line by line while it works.

Everything happens on your device. Your audio is never uploaded, there is no account, and transcription keeps working in airplane mode once a speech model is downloaded.

WHY EXTRACTER
• Any format: MP3, M4A, WAV, OGG, OPUS, FLAC, AAC, MP4, MOV, MKV, WEBM, AVI and many more. Video files work too; the audio track is used automatically.
• 100% offline and private: speech recognition runs on your phone with OpenAI Whisper models. Nothing leaves the device.
• About 100 languages with automatic language detection, plus an option to translate speech into English.
• Live results: see the text as it is recognised, with progress for every file.
• Batch queue: add several files at once and let them run one after another.
• Timestamps for every sentence.
• Copy as clean paragraphs, or with timestamps.
• Export to TXT, SRT or VTT subtitles and share anywhere.
• Full-text search across all your transcripts.

CHOOSE YOUR MODEL
Download a free model once, from Tiny (about 30 MB, fastest) to Large v3 Turbo (about 550 MB, most accurate). Switch at any time and delete the ones you no longer need.

GREAT FOR
Students recording lectures, journalists and researchers transcribing interviews, teams keeping notes of meetings, creators making subtitles, and anyone who would rather read than rewind.

No subscriptions, no ads, no sign-up.
